# Manual Listening Checklist — Phase 4

Listen for several minutes per condition. Use headphones, laptop speakers, and external speakers where possible.

## Stillness

- [ ] No clicks or pops on note starts
- [ ] No unnecessary percussion
- [ ] Comfortable silence between phrases
- [ ] Pad remains warm and non-harsh

## Rain (Light Rain mood)

- [ ] Plucks and textures stay subtle
- [ ] No excessive high-frequency ticking
- [ ] Delay diffuse, not muddy

## Snow

- [ ] Sparse and quiet overall level
- [ ] No bright harshness
- [ ] Long tails controlled, not infinite mud

## Wind (Strong Wind)

- [ ] Energy increases gradually
- [ ] Stereo motion subtle, not seasick
- [ ] Bass stable and centred

## Storm

- [ ] Powerful but not painfully loud
- [ ] Impacts remain rare
- [ ] No clipping (peak meter below 0.92)
- [ ] Note density controlled, not chaotic

## Recovery

- [ ] Layers fade gradually after storm
- [ ] Release tails audible through transitions
- [ ] Harmony settles naturally

## General

- [ ] 60+ minute session without crashes or runaway volume
- [ ] Recording WAV matches live output (processed, limited)
- [ ] Soundscape preset changes crossfade smoothly
- [ ] Quality Low/Standard/High audible difference without musical change
